# Workspace Management

Keeping your team’s workspace organized is key to smooth testing. To manage your workspace settings, navigate to Settings > General from your Rova Mobile dashboard.

Here, you will find your Workspace General Settings, which are split into two main sections: your Workspace Profile and the Danger Zone.

### Workspace Profile

Your Workspace Profile is where you manage the identity of your collaborative testing environment.

#### Changing Your Workspace Name

If your team rebrands or you just want to make the workspace name more descriptive, you can update it instantly:

1. Go to **Settings > General**.
2. Locate the **Workspace Profile** section.
3. Type your new workspace name into the text field.
4. Click Save Changes (or Update).

_Note: Changing the workspace name will update it for all invited members, but it won't affect your active API keys or automated test runs._

### Danger Zone

The Danger Zone contains sensitive actions that cannot be undone. Please proceed with caution here.

#### Deleting Your Workspace

If you no longer need your Rova Mobile workspace (for example, if a project has wrapped up permanently), you can delete it:

1. Scroll down to the bottom of the General settings page to locate the Danger Zone.
2. Click Delete Workspace.
3. You will be prompted to type in the name of your workspace to confirm the deletion.
4. Confirm the action to permanently remove the workspace.

> ⚠️ Warning: Deleting a workspace is permanent and irreversible. Doing so will instantly and permanently delete all of your uploaded mobile apps, test histories, execution plans, API keys, and team member accesses.

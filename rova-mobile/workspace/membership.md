# Membership & Roles Management

Manage who has access to your Rova Mobile workspace and what they can do.

### Role Definitions

| **Role** | **Ideal for...**                                                                                              |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Owner    | The person responsible for the Rova Mobile account and billing.                                               |
| Admin    | Mobile leads or Release/DevOps engineers who need to manage device configurations, integrations, and members. |
| Viewer   | Stakeholders (Product Managers, Designers) who only need to review device logs and test run results.          |

### Managing Members

#### Adding Members

Invite team members via email. If they don't have a Rova account yet, they will be prompted to create one to access the mobile workspace.

#### Changing Roles

Owners and Admins can update a member's role at any time from the **Members list**. Changes take effect immediately.

#### Removing Members

When a member leaves the team, remove them from the workspace to instantly revoke their access to your mobile test suites, API keys, and project data.

### Recommendations

* Minimize Owners: Keep the number of Owners small (usually 1-2 people).
* Default to Editor: Most technical team members, such as iOS and Android developers, should be Editors.
* Use Viewers for external partners: If you work with external mobile agencies, QA contractors, or business stakeholders, the Viewer role is the safest way to share your testing progress.
